Modules & Features

📋 Item Master

  • Item creation with unique codes

  • Unit of measurement (UOM) selection
  • Selling rate aur cost price
  • Tax classification (GST slab)
  • Item images aur specifications
  • Supplier mapping aur reorder level

🏭 Warehouse Management

Multi-warehouse inventory tracking
  • Multiple warehouse creation aur management
  • Real-time stock levels by warehouse
  • Stock transfer between warehouses
  • Bin/Shelf location tracking
  • Stock aging report
  • Physical stock verification aur reconciliation
  • Low stock alerts aur reorder management

🛒 Purchase Management

Supplier order aur receipt tracking
  • Supplier master creation
  • Purchase request (RFQ) generation
  • Purchase order creation aur tracking
  • Purchase order status (Draft, Submitted, Received, Completed)
  • Goods receipt note (GRN) creation
  • Supplier invoice matching (3-way match)

📊 Stock Transactions

Track all inventory movements aur adjustments
  • Purchase receipt (inward)
  • Sales delivery (outward)
  • Stock adjustment (damage, loss, gain)
  • Internal transfer transactions
  • Stock movement history aur traceability
  • FIFO/LIFO valuation method
  • Batch/Serial number tracking

Accounts & Billing

Complete financial management — invoicing se lekar GST compliance tak.

Modules & Features

🧾 Sales Invoicing

Generate aur manage sales invoices with GST
  • Sales invoice creation from sales order
  • Invoice numbering (INV series with auto-increment)
  • Multi-line item support with quantity & rate
  • Automatic GST @18% calculation
  • Subtotal, GST, aur grand total automation
  • Invoice status tracking (Draft, Submitted, Paid, Cancelled)
  • Invoice templates aur customization

💸 Expense Tracking

Record aur categorize business expenses
  • Expense claim creation by employees
  • Expense categorization (Travel, Meals, Office, etc.)
  • Receipt upload aur documentation
  • Approval workflow (Employee → Manager → Finance)
  • Expense reimbursement processing
  • Monthly expense summary reports
  • Vendor bill management

💳 Payment & Collections

Track receivables aur payables
  • Payment receipt creation
  • Payment mode selection (Cash, Cheque, Bank Transfer, Card)
  • Invoice payment reconciliation
  • Outstanding receivables aging report
  • Payment due date tracking aur reminders
  • Partial payment support
  • Payment term management (Net 30, Net 60, etc.)

📊 Financial Reports

Generate financial statements aur analysis
  • Profit & Loss (P&L) statement
  • Balance sheet generation
  • Cash flow statement
  • Trial balance aur general ledger
  • GST summary (IGST, CGST, SGST breakdown)
  • Revenue by customer analysis
  • Expense breakdown by category

📋 GST Compliance

Automatic GST calculation aur reporting
  • GST rate selection (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%)
  • HSN/SAC code mapping
  • IGST/CGST/SGST auto-split
  • GST summary for returns
  • GSTR-1, GSTR-2 readiness
  • Tax liability calculation
  • Input tax credit tracking

📚 Chart of Accounts

General ledger aur account management
  • Account head creation (Assets, Liabilities, Income, Expenses)
  • Account code aur hierarchical structure
  • Manual journal entry creation
  • Debit/Credit posting
  • Account reconciliation
  • Account history aur audit trail
  • Opening balance entry
